RC Ellis is a British indie author writing regional horror, AI and cyber comic thrillers with authentic voices and bone-dry wit. His horror fiction moves away from the usual London-centred apocalypse, asking what happens when disaster reaches the isolated South West, where tractors, fishing boats and cut-off communities become part of the fight for survival.

His newer fiction turns that same dry humour towards technology, AI and cyber security, exploring what happens when intelligent systems are given simple instructions, bad assumptions and far too much freedom. Whether the threat is undead or digital, his stories focus on ordinary people dealing with very unusual problems.

# Isles of Scilly

#### About

**They don't shamble. They coordinate. They hunt.**

A cancer cure that promised miracles instead rewrote human DNA, creating the Connected, infected who move as one, adapting, learning, spreading. Within days, Britain fell silent.

Sarah fled to the Isles of Scilly with her children, hoping twenty-eight miles of cold Atlantic would be enough. But isolation cuts both ways. Resources dwindle. Tensions rise. And the Connected are evolving.

When survival means impossible choices, how much of your humanity can you sacrifice before you become the thing you're running from?

A relentless British thriller set on the Isles of Scilly for fans of The Girl With All The Gifts and World War Z.

RC Ellis is the author of Cornwall, a British horror bestseller. Isles of Scilly delivers his signature blend of visceral tension, authentic regional voice, and characters worth fighting for.

# Cornwall

#### About

Callum thought debugging code in an isolated Cornish barn was as bad as life got.

Elliott figured waking up before noon was a genuine crisis.

Jessica’s mindfulness retreat was supposed to save her sanity.

Lisa just wanted a peaceful life.

And Tomo? He’d be happier if the tourists stayed off his farm.

But when a strange infection begins to spread across Cornwall, turning everyday people into red-eyed, feral infected, the quiet life becomes a fight for survival. Thrown together by fate, sarcasm, and sheer desperation, this unlikely group must battle wave after wave of undead in a county that suddenly isn’t so sleepy anymore.

When the dead rise in Cornwall, even a peaceful life comes with killer complications.
